1 / A physical beginning

A switch controlled by a signal #

A MOS transistor has a gate terminal and a conducting path between source and drain. Voltage at the gate controls that path. Here we idealize it as a switch: either conducting or open.

An nMOS switch conducts for a high input; a pMOS switch conducts for a low input. Pair them between the supply and ground, and their shared output gives the opposite of the input.

Physical basis: Computation Structures, CMOS. Real voltage thresholds, capacitance, leakage, and switching delays are outside this ideal digital model.
