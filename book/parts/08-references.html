Reading & references

How this book is taking shape #

Distill is the editorial reference: a continuous argument with figures the reader can manipulate. The mathematical references motivate compact definitions, explicit hypotheses, and careful separation of a formal theory from the structures it describes. This book develops its own text and constructions.

  1. Hohman, Conlen, Heer & Chau. Communicating with Interactive Articles. Distill, 2020.
  2. Roman Kossak & James Schmerl. The Structure of Models of Peano Arithmetic. Oxford University Press, 2006. A reference for the theory–model distinction, not a claim that a finite word machine models Peano arithmetic.
  3. Walter Rudin. Principles of Mathematical Analysis, 3rd ed. McGraw-Hill, 1976. A reference for the definition–theorem–proof progression toward analysis; its classical foundations are not imported without examination.
  4. Computation Structures: CMOS and Sequential Logic. Electrical and circuit background.
  5. Kurt Gödel. Über formal unentscheidbare Sätze der Principia Mathematica und verwandter Systeme I, 1931.
  6. Oracle Numerical Computation Guide: Associative Operations.
  7. Andrej Bauer & Iztok Kavkler. Implementing real numbers with RZ, 2007.

The next construction should earn its place: define it, implement it, prove what we can, and account for the work.
