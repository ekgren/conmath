2 / From voltage to a rule

Two values, then logic #

Definition 2.1 · Bit. A Bit has exactly two values, 0 and 1, also called low and high. Equality compares these values. The operation not exchanges them.

A NAND gate returns 0 only when both inputs are 1. In CMOS, the pull-down uses two nMOS switches in series; the pull-up uses two pMOS switches in parallel. Both inputs must be high to connect the output to ground.

Tie the inputs together and NAND becomes NOT. Invert a NAND output and we obtain AND. Invert both inputs before a NAND and we obtain OR. These identities let us assemble more complex finite logic.

not(a)    = NAND(a, a)
and(a, b) = not(NAND(a, b))
or(a, b)  = NAND(not(a), not(b))
